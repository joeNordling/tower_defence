Factions — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 20 April 2026

Factions ("the Game") is a tower defense game developed and published by Running Man Games ("we", "us"). This policy describes what information the Game collects, how that information is used, and the rights you have regarding your information.

Summary

Factions is designed to minimise the personal information it collects. We don't run our own ad network, sell your data, or ask you to create an account. Third-party platforms (Google Play, AdMob, Play Games Services) each collect data under their own policies when you use those features. Game progress stays on your device unless you explicitly sign into Play Games Services, in which case Google syncs your game state to your Google account.

Information we collect

1. Gameplay telemetry

The Game sends anonymous gameplay events to our server (signal.streamingsplats.com) to help us understand which modes and features are being used. These events include:

  • Match starts and ends (mode, faction, map, difficulty, wave reached, duration, win/loss)
  • Faction selection
  • Multiplayer session starts (mode, player count)

These events do not contain your name, email, Google ID, device identifier, or IP address. You can opt out of telemetry at any time by clearing browser data or through the Settings menu (where available).

2. Google AdMob (advertising)

The Game displays occasional ads supplied by Google AdMob. When you interact with an ad, AdMob may collect:

  • Your device's advertising ID (a user-resettable identifier)
  • Approximate location derived from your IP address
  • Ad interaction data (impressions, clicks, video completions)
  • Device information (model, operating system, app version)

This data is collected by Google under its Privacy Policy. You can limit ad personalisation through your device settings (Android: Settings → Google → Ads). Users who purchase the "Remove Ads" in-app upgrade stop seeing interstitials and banners entirely, though rewarded ads remain available as an optional reward path.

3. Google Play Games Services

If you choose to sign in with Google Play Games Services, Google provides the Game with:

  • Your Play Games display name
  • A stable player ID specific to Factions
  • Your Play Games profile avatar URL (when available)

We use this information to display your identity in-game, submit achievements and leaderboard scores, and (in future) synchronise game progress across devices through Google's Saved Games service. Play Games Services data is governed by the Google Privacy Policy.

4. In-app purchases (Google Play Billing)

When you make a purchase, the transaction is handled entirely by Google Play Billing. Google collects and processes your payment information — we never see your credit card, billing address, or Google account email. The Game receives only a confirmation that the purchase was successful plus the product identifier of what you bought (e.g. "ads_off", "skin_pack_arcane_corrupted"), which we use to unlock the corresponding content.

5. Local game state

Your game progress — owned skins, shard balance, high scores, settings, tutorial completion, ad cooldown timestamps — is stored in your device's local app storage. This information never leaves your device unless you sign into Play Games Services (which syncs game state to your Google account via Google's Saved Games service) or restore purchases (which queries Google Play for your past transactions).

6. Multiplayer (WebRTC peer-to-peer)

Versus and Circle Co-op multiplayer modes use a peer-to-peer connection between your device and your opponent's/teammates' devices. Our signalling server briefly relays connection handshake data (session description and network candidate information) to let peers find each other; once connected, gameplay data flows directly between devices. Peer IP addresses are visible to the other players in the match — this is inherent to peer-to-peer architecture. We do not log or store session data on the signalling server.

Information we do NOT collect

  • Your name, email address, phone number, or physical address
  • Your Google account email (Play Games provides a separate stable ID)
  • Your payment information (handled entirely by Google Play)
  • Contacts, calendar, photos, or files from your device
  • Location beyond the approximate IP-based location AdMob uses for ad targeting
  • Microphone, camera, or any continuous sensor data

How we use information

We use the gameplay telemetry described above to:

  • Understand which modes and features are being used most
  • Fix bugs by identifying where players are running into problems
  • Balance game difficulty based on aggregate win rates

We do not use your information for advertising, do not sell your information to third parties, and do not combine it with any external dataset about you.

Data retention

Anonymous telemetry events are retained indefinitely in aggregate form but contain no information that can be traced back to an individual user. If you uninstall the Game, local game state on your device is deleted by the operating system.

Children's privacy

Factions is rated for ages 13 and up. It is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete the information as required by COPPA and similar laws.

Your rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the right to:

  • Request information about what data we hold about you
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Opt out of telemetry collection
  • Limit personalised advertising through your device settings

For requests relating to Google Play Games Services, Play Billing, or AdMob data, please use Google's account tools at myaccount.google.com.

For requests relating to telemetry we collect, contact us using the details below.
